Step 1 of 2

Before you turn it on

  • What it does. When you close the last tab of a site, Dustpan waits 30 seconds and then deletes that site's cookies.
  • You will be signed out. Every site that is not on your keep list forgets you once it has been cleaned. That is what a cookie cleaner is for. In the next step you pick the sites you want to stay signed in to.
  • What it never touches. A site that is still open in any tab, window or container, and any site on your keep list.
  • A mistake can be undone. For 15 minutes after a clean, Restore puts the cookies back. After that they are gone for good.
  • Nothing is deleted when you turn it on. Cleaning starts with the next tab you close.

        Settings

        The time between closing a site's last tab and deleting its cookies. Reopen the site within that time and nothing is deleted.

        Off: a tab holds on to every site it has shown until you close the tab, so going back and signing in through another site keep working. On: a site is cleaned as soon as no tab is showing it. A sign-in that sends you to another site for longer than the wait can then fail.

        Off: closing Firefox with tabs open cleans nothing. On: about a minute after Firefox starts, those sites are cleaned, unless their tabs came back or you keep them.

        Good to know

        • What a site is. mail.example.com and accounts.example.com are one site, example.com. Keeping or cleaning always applies to the whole site.
        • Kept until Firefox closes. The site stays signed in while Firefox is open and is cleaned about a minute after the next start, unless it is open again by then.
        • Embedded content. Cookies that Firefox stores for a site's embedded content (Total Cookie Protection) belong to the site you visited, and are kept or cleaned together with it.
        • Containers. A site is cleaned in all containers at once, and only when it is open in none of them.
        • Private windows. Dustpan does not run there. Firefox deletes private cookies itself when the last private window closes.
        • Cookies only. Other things a site may store in your browser, such as local storage, are not touched in this version.
        • On your device. Dustpan sends nothing anywhere. The keep list, the settings and the activity list stay in this Firefox.
