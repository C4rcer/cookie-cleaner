Harbour bridge reopens after three weeks of repairs

The swing bridge at the east quay carried its first traffic again on Thursday morning, two days ahead of schedule. The harbour office says the new bearings should last thirty years.

Ferry passengers no longer need the detour over the lock, and the Saturday market returns to its usual place on the quay.
